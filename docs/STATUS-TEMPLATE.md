## Door rules

- Amber HOLD means the floor is up and the gate is shut.
- The firing bar is +80 bps of expected edge after costs, on paper and on live. Under the bar, no order.
- Put a cash figure on the door only if it was read this session. Name the book it belongs to.
- Do not put a withdraw, transfer, or deposit control on the door.
- Do not invent P&L, hit rate, Sharpe, volume, or an equity curve.
